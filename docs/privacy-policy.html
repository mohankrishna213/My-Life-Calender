FOCUS · GOAL & HABIT TRACKER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Focus: Goal & Habit Tracker (“Focus”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android application. Focus is designed as a local-first app. Based on the current version of the app, your goal and task data is stored on your Android device and the app does not operate a user account or its own backend service.

In short: Focus does not require an account, does not ask you for your name or email address, and does not currently send your goals or tasks to a Focus server.

1. Information stored in the app

Focus lets you create a goal with a title, start date, and end date, and create tasks and subtasks associated with dates. This information is stored locally on your device. The current implementation uses SharedPreferences, with encrypted preferences used when the Android Keystore is available.

The app may also store local preferences describing whether notifications are enabled and whether the optional lock-screen wallpaper has been applied, along with the last local application time. These are used only to provide the corresponding features.

2. Information we collect

The current version of Focus does not collect personal information through a Focus account or server. We do not currently operate analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs in the app.

Because Focus does not have an account or cloud-sync service, we do not receive your goal titles, task titles, completion history, or dates from the app as part of normal use.

3. Notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, Android may ask for the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission on supported versions of Android. Focus uses notifications for local task reminders and motivational prompts. The app schedules this work on your device and does not need to send your task data to a remote notification service.

You can turn Focus notifications off in the app’s Settings or through Android system settings.

4. Lock-screen wallpaper

Focus includes an optional lock-screen wallpaper feature. When you choose to apply it, the app generates a wallpaper on your device from your goal, dates, task completion, and progress. It then uses Android’s wallpaper APIs to apply the generated image.

The wallpaper feature can be removed from within the app. The app also refreshes an already-applied wallpaper locally when relevant goal or task information changes.

5. Device permissions

The current Android package declares permissions for notifications, wallpaper access, and receiving the device boot-completed event. The boot event is used by the wallpaper component to support local wallpaper behavior after a device restart. Focus does not use these permissions to create an account or upload your personal content.

6. Data sharing

We do not currently sell, rent, or share your Focus goal and task content with advertisers or data brokers. There is no Focus backend receiving this content in the current implementation.

If future versions introduce analytics, advertising, cloud synchronization, account features, or another third-party service that changes how data is collected or shared, this policy will be updated before or when that functionality is introduced, as applicable.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your Focus goal and task data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Because the current version has no Focus account or cloud database, we do not maintain a server-side copy that you need to request us to delete.

Android’s app-data controls can be used to clear locally stored app data. If you need help with data stored by a particular device configuration, privacy questions or deletion-related assistance can be submitted through the project’s public GitHub repository.

8. Security

Focus is designed to keep its core data local. Where supported by the device, the app uses AndroidX EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by Android Keystore cryptography for its stored goal and task data. The implementation also includes a local fallback for devices where the Keystore-backed storage cannot be initialized; therefore, we do not claim that every device provides identical storage protection.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and you should use the security controls provided by your Android device.

9. Children’s privacy

Focus is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account or request a child’s personal information for normal use. If you believe personal information has been submitted to us, please contact us through the project’s public support channel.

10. Third-party services and links

The current app does not require third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud-storage services for its core functionality. This website may link to external services such as Google Play or GitHub. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Focus changes or when legal, platform, or operational requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest version. Material changes will be reflected here before the updated policy is relied on for the affected functionality, where appropriate.

12. Privacy contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns about Focus, use the project’s public GitHub repository and open an issue describing your request without including unnecessary personal information. The repository is maintained by the developer named in the project: Mohan Krishna.
